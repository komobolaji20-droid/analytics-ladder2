What It Is

A free WhatsApp community for aspiring analysts

The Analytics Ladder started as a simple idea: bring together Nigerians who want to break into data analytics, and give them a clear, structured, honest path — for free. It lives right now as a WhatsApp community, where members learn together, ask questions, and hold each other accountable.

It isn't a course you buy and forget. It's a group of real people, at different stages of the same climb, supporting each other rung by rung.

How We Got Here

Built from one honest realization

"You can't be piling up certificates without projects to show for it."

That single realization is the whole reason The Analytics Ladder exists. Too many beginners collect course completion certificates and still can't get hired — because a certificate only proves you sat through a video. It doesn't prove you can actually do the work.

So this community was built around a different promise: every member works through real, practical exercises and builds real projects — things they can put on GitHub, LinkedIn, and their portfolio, and actually show a recruiter.

Where We Are Now

Growing, one cohort at a time

Our first group of members is already climbing — working through a structured, zero-to-job-ready path covering Excel, SQL, Power BI, and Python, with GitHub and job-readiness built in from day one. This website is the next step: turning that same structured path into something every member can learn from at their own pace, with guidance from me along the way.
