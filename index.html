My Story

From the classroom to the dashboard

My name is Omobolaji Kehinde Zachariah most people just call me Zachariah. I currently teach at Amao Group of Schools in Ibadan, Nigeria, and I'm also building a career as a Data Analyst and BI Specialist.

I know exactly what it feels like to start from zero. Moving from teaching into data wasn't something that happened overnight it took real, patient learning, one skill at a time. That's exactly why I care so much about how this platform teaches: step-by-step, in plain language, with nothing assumed and nothing skipped.

Online, you'll usually find me under the name #TeacherTurnedAnalyst sharing what I'm learning, building in public across LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Facebook, and Instagram, and breaking down complex data topics the same way I'd break them down for a classroom of students.

Why I Teach This Way

If a topic feels complicated, I haven't explained it simply enough yet.

Step by step, always

Every lesson on this site is built the way I'd teach a classroom breaking big ideas into small, clear pieces, so nothing feels overwhelming.

Projects over certificates

I built this platform on one hard lesson: certificates without real projects don't get anyone hired. Every part of this course pushes you toward something you can actually show.
